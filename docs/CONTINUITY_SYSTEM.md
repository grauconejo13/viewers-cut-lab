# Continuity and Story-Logic System

## Purpose

After creator approval and opening-scene generation, the lab produces one
reviewable continuity and story-logic analysis. It identifies risks for a human
creator; it does not automatically rewrite, publish, or guarantee a screenplay.

## Analysis domains

The Continuity Supervisor checks props and ownership, prop condition, costumes,
injuries, and locations. The Story Logic Reviewer checks character knowledge,
motivations, chronology, world rules, setups and payoffs, unresolved threads,
contradictions, and potential plot holes.

## Proposed finding format

Each finding identifies its domain, severity, evidence, affected story elements,
and a suggested question or resolution. A clear result may say no issue was
found in the supplied material; it must not claim exhaustive verification.

## Workflow boundary

Analysis uses the approved audience-development brief, approved story
assumptions, and generated opening scene. It returns a review artifact after
scene generation; the creator remains the authority on edits and acceptance.
